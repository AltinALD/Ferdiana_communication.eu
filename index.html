The practice

A clarity practice for people with serious work.

Ferdiana is a social scientist. Her PhD at the University of Antwerp studied how uncertainty is communicated when the stakes are high. She has carried that discipline through a research centre, a university classroom, industry learning, and a public practice in science communication.

The brief is steady: researchers need ideas to become funded projects, and complex work needs to stay accurate once it leaves the specialist circle. A session finds the sentence that matters, then the structure that can carry it.

Also in the background: Communication and Learning Manager at Umicore, PhD researcher at SCK CEN, and founder of the outreach series Stand-Up Scientist. She is Communications Officer and a board member of the Society for Risk Analysis Europe.

Approach

    Online, in Antwerp, or in the room where the team already works.
